What is a node?

A node is a small ESP32-C5 board that you plug into the CardputerZero by USB. It listens for Wi-Fi on 2.4 and 5 GHz, and for Bluetooth when you ask, and tells Quartermaster what it hears. Quartermaster adds where you are and writes the log. A node can also run on a battery away from the CardputerZero and send what it hears by radio.

Flash a board

  1. Plug the board into this computer or phone with a USB cable that carries data.
  2. Press a button below and pick the board in the list that opens.
  3. Wait until it says it is done, then plug the board into the CardputerZero.

Boards

  • Seeed XIAO ESP32-C5
  • Espressif ESP32-C5-DevKitC-1
  • M5Stack Stamp-C5
  • Other ESP32-C5 boards with 4 MB of flash or more

If the board is not found

Put the board in download mode, then press the button again.

Seeed XIAO ESP32-C5

Hold the BOOT button while you plug the board in. Let go once it is in.

M5Stack Stamp-C5

Hold pin G28 to GND while you plug the board in. Let go once it is in.

ESP32-C5-DevKitC-1

Hold the BOOT button, press and let go of RESET, then let go of BOOT. The board has two USB-C ports; either one works.

Still not found? Try another cable, as some only charge. Close other programs and tabs that use the board.

Browsers that work

  • Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac, Linux or a Chromebook
  • Chrome on Android, with a USB-C cable or an OTG adapter

Safari, and every browser on iPhone or iPad, cannot flash a board. If the button says this browser cannot flash, open the page in Chrome or Edge.

Flash from Quartermaster instead

Quartermaster comes with this firmware too. Plug the board into the CardputerZero and open Settings > Flash a node. It can also update the nodes you have added.
